WebSerial Firmware Flasher for ESP32-C3 & ESP32-S3

🚀 1-Click Web Installation

Flash the firmware directly from your browser to your ESP32-C3 (LilyGO T-01C3) or ESP32-S3. No software, drivers, or toolchains required on your PC (just Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera).

⚠️ Your browser does not support the Web Serial API. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on desktop.

Tip: After connecting, you can also view Logs & Console via the same button.

Installation Steps:

  1. Connect the board to your computer using a USB-to-UART adapter.
  2. Bootloader Mode (LilyGO T-01C3 / ESP-01): Connect GPIO9 (Pin 5 / BOOT) to GND while powering on or resetting the adapter if your programmer does not toggle DTR/RTS automatically.
  3. Click "Connect & Flash Device" above and select your serial COM port from the browser prompt.
  4. For a first-time installation, check "Erase device" to completely wipe the flash, then start the installation.
  5. After a successful flash, the ESP32 restarts and broadcasts the setup WiFi network ESP-HeatMeter-Setup (IP: 192.168.4.1).
